Privacy policy

This is my personal website. Here is everything it does with your data, in plain language.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version

  • Reading the site needs nothing from you: no account, no cookies, and no tracking unless you allow it.
  • With your permission, Vercel Web Analytics counts visits and Speed Insights measures how fast the page loads, without cookies. You can say no and change your mind at any time.
  • If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are passed through Web3Forms to my inbox.
  • Fonts and everything else on the page come from this domain. Nothing is loaded from Google or any other third party.

2. Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA) and delivered from its servers around the world. When you open a page, Vercel processes your IP address, the time, the page you asked for, the referring page and your browser's user-agent string, in order to deliver the page and to protect it against attacks. These logs are kept only briefly and are not used to identify you.

Vercel is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognises as providing adequate protection (Art. 45 GDPR).

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Visitor statistics, only with your consent

If you click “Allow” in the bar at the bottom of the page, two Vercel tools are switched on:

  • Vercel Web Analytics counts page views. For each one it records the time, the page address, the referring site, your approximate location (country, region, city, derived from your IP address), your operating system, your browser and the type of device.
  • Vercel Speed Insights measures how fast the page loads and responds (the “Core Web Vitals”), together with the page address, your browser, device type, operating system, connection speed and country.

Neither tool sets cookies or stores anything in your browser. To tell visits apart, Web Analytics computes a hash from your request, which is discarded after 24 hours. I only see the totals, never individual visitors. The data is processed by Vercel Inc. (USA) under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). If you click “No thanks” or make no choice, neither tool is loaded.

Your current choice:

You can withdraw your consent at any time, here or under “Privacy settings” at the bottom of the home page. Withdrawing does not affect anything collected before (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

4. Contact form and email

The contact form asks for your name, your email address and your message, and also sends the language of the page. When you press “Send message”, your browser transmits this, together with your IP address, to Web3Forms (operated by Web3Creative, Kerala, India), which forwards it to my email inbox. According to its data processing agreement, Web3Forms works with sub-processors such as Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare, Hetzner and spam filters (Akismet, CleanTalk), whose servers are in the USA, the EU and elsewhere, and may keep a copy of submissions for up to three years. Because India and the USA as a whole are not covered by an EU adequacy decision, this transfer relies on the EU standard contractual clauses that are part of that agreement (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

My inbox is my email account at the Technical University of Munich (TUM). If you would rather not use the form, write to me directly at ; the message then only goes through the email providers involved.

I use what you send only to answer you. I delete the message once your request has been dealt with, unless I need it for something still ongoing, such as an application process.

Legal basis: taking steps you asked for before a possible contract, for example about a job (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); otherwise my legitimate interest in answering messages sent to me (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Links to other websites

The site links to my profiles on GitHub and LinkedIn and to the live versions of my projects. Those sites are only contacted when you click a link, and their own privacy policies apply from then on. Wanderly and TruckOps each have their own privacy page.
